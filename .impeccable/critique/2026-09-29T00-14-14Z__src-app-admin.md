### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The admin speaks in the school's own voice — advisory signatories, draft/publish, ordered lists, monospaced dates and order numbers, and the "Official Record" uppercase heading system. That's genuinely authored, not interchangeable in tone. Structurally, though, it's a conventional small-CMS skeleton (header, left nav, card lists, two-column edit forms) that any institution could wear. The bespoke moments are real but few: conditional signatory fields on the news form, the Notice Stripe on the dashboard's latest advisory, and the read-only "computed from the visible faculty list" stat.

### Overall Impression

This is a considered, honest admin, noticeably better than most small-school CMS panels: it respects focus management, labels its fields, and keeps the public design system's discipline. The two things it doesn't do are help a busy staffer **find** an existing record, and protect against **accidentally publishing** one. Those are the highest-value fixes.

### What's Working

1. **The two-step delete** (`src/components/admin/confirm-delete-button.tsx:52`) announces via `role="alert"`, focuses Cancel, supports Escape, and returns focus to the trigger. That's a level of care most commercial CRUD never reaches.
2. **Progressive disclosure on the news form** (`src/app/admin/(dashboard)/news/news-form.tsx:198`): signatory fields appear only for Advisory; publishing controls live in a distinct sidebar; a sticky action bar serves narrow screens — a clean Operate-mode composition.
3. **Validation and recovery**: inline field errors with `aria-describedby`/`aria-invalid`, values preserved on server rejection (`state.values`), an in-shell error boundary with a reference digest, and an in-shell 404.

### Priority Issues

**[P1] Lists have no retrieval tools**
- **What**: News, Faculty, and Testimonials render every record in one flat list. No search, no status/category filter, no sort, no pagination.
- **Why it matters**: Every real list grows. Staff will scroll and squint to find one advisory, and drafts/published posts are visually mixed — the exact task ("find the post from last week") the admin exists for gets slow and error-prone.
- **Fix**: Add a list toolbar with search, status and category filters, and count-aware result feedback; group or filter drafts. Keep the existing card row styling.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable shape`

**[P1] New posts go live by default**
- **What**: On a new post, `isPublished` defaults to checked (`news-form.tsx:261-265`), and the primary button says "Create post" — one click publishes to the official public site. The list's Publish/Unpublish toggles also act instantly with no confirmation or undo.
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md says approvability outranks flourish; an official record should not publish a half-written advisory because a checkbox was already ticked. This is the single riskiest interaction in the admin.
- **Fix**: Default new posts to draft; make publishing an explicit choice ("Save draft" primary, "Publish" a distinct action), and consider a confirm step for unpublishing.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Settings is a wall of ungrouped fields**
- **What**: `settings/page.tsx` drops two forms straight onto the page — Site identity alone is nine fields in one 2-column grid (name, short name, tagline, description, address, phone, email, social repeater) with no card and no subgroups. Every editor form elsewhere wraps grouped controls in a bordered card.
- **Why it matters**: Visual hierarchy collapses; users can't scan "what belongs together," and it breaks the container language the rest of the admin established.
- **Fix**: Chunk into Identity / Contact / Social links fieldsets inside cards; keep one save per section or add a sticky save.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Edit pages lack wayfinding and record identity**
- **What**: About and Academics pages open with "← About content" / "← Academics content" links. News, Faculty, and Testimonials edit pages show only "Edit post" / "Edit member" (`news/[id]/page.tsx:23`, `faculty/[id]/page.tsx:23`) — no back link, and the header never names the record being edited.
- **Why it matters**: For non-technical staff this creates a small but recurring "where am I, which record is this?" moment, forcing a scan of form fields to confirm.
- **Fix**: Standardize a back link to the section and surface the record title/name in the edit header.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Form plumbing is duplicated six-plus ways**
- **What**: `FieldError` is re-implemented in seven places (`news-form.tsx:337`, `site-settings-form.tsx:204`, `school-stats-form.tsx:109`, both `list-form.tsx` files, `faculty-form.tsx:233`, `testimonial-form.tsx:155`); the select class string is copy-pasted four times; `AboutListForm` and `AcademicsListForm` are near-identical twins that have already diverged.
- **Why it matters**: Visual drift is inevitable — the next error-style or focus-ring change must be made in seven files, and one will be missed.
- **Fix**: Extract shared `FormField`, `Select`, and `FieldError` primitives (and unify the two list forms) into `src/components/admin/`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

### Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)**: No search or filter on any list — finding last week's advisory is a manual scan. No bulk publish/feature/delete. No keyboard save (Cmd/Ctrl+S) on the long news form. Every publish toggle is a full server round-trip; on a rural connection that's a slow, silent-feeling interaction.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: Mostly a strong pass — skip link, real labels, `aria-describedby`/`aria-invalid`, `role="alert"` errors, visible focus rings, focus restoration on delete, sr-only loading text. Flags: the mobile nav is an `overflow-x-auto` strip with seven items and no visible scroll cue (keyboard users may never reach "Site settings"); pending state changes ("Saving…") aren't announced via a live region; the delete confirmation's label is visually truncated to 14rem with no way to reveal the full title on hover/focus.

**Mila (School office staff — derived from PRODUCT.md's approval/operating context)**: First-timer with low-to-moderate digital literacy. "Excerpt" is unexplained jargon; the pre-checked "Published" box is exactly the kind of detail she won't notice before clicking "Create post"; after saving, the redirect to the list gives no "saved" acknowledgement; she can't tell from the edit header which record she opened when several advisories share similar titles.

### Minor Observations

- `image-upload.tsx:95` — "Clear" empties the URL but doesn't reset the file input; re-selecting the same file afterwards won't fire `change`.
- `loading.tsx` is a list-shaped skeleton reused for form-heavy pages (Settings), where it doesn't match the coming layout.
- List titles use `truncate` with no `title` attribute — long advisory titles can't be read without opening them.
- Posts show `publishedOn` but no "last updated" metadata, despite DESIGN.md elevating dated/attributable metadata as a core principle.
- The login page has no link back to the public site, and shows no school logo — it's the one admin surface that doesn't carry the brand.
- `destructive` delete buttons use red, which DESIGN.md says to avoid for error states "if possible"; for irreversible deletes it's defensible, but the pattern was chosen consciously elsewhere (blue `aria-invalid` borders).

### Questions to Consider

- What would a confident version of this admin look like if "find the record" were treated as the primary task, not an afterthought?
- Should the admin's austere, all-caps documentary tone soften for a non-technical school office user, or is severity part of the trust argument?
- If publishing were two deliberate steps, would staff publish *better* advisories?
- Could the dashboard answer "what needs my attention today?" instead of listing six equal entrances?
